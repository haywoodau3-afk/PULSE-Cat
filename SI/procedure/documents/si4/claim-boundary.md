# Claim boundary

The package supports retrospective computational statements about fixed-domain
progressive ordering, reciprocal transfer diagnostics, and conditional use of
pose features under the declared catalyst and aryl-pathway contract.

It does not support claims of wet-lab acceleration, autonomous closed-loop
chemistry, universal catalyst transfer, GNN superiority, experimental
validation of generated candidates, supplier availability, or laboratory
safety. The 10% early-AUC transfer superiority gate remains unresolved; report
directional gains and negative-transfer suppression with their paired
uncertainty rather than promoting a universal transfer claim.

Stage 2p/P3/P4 pose effects are conditional. Stage 2pp occupancy fields are
geometric diagnostics, not electron density. The generated 8+8 panel is
prediction-only and remains outcome-masked.
