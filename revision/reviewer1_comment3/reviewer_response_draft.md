# Draft response to Reviewer 1 comment 3

We thank the reviewer for pointing out that our earlier analyses did not directly assess the relationship between representation and acquisition. We have added a matched factorial retrospective comparison crossing structure-only features or structure plus all 248 Stage 2p fields with source-order continuation or uncertainty–diversity acquisition. The comparisons use the same 20 held-out splits, three starting pairs per split and five label budgets in each catalyst system; three random continuations per pair provide a secondary comparator. Ridge is used for held-out prediction, and the uncertainty component of acquisition uses the representation in its candidate predictions.

The mean held-out MAEs for structure/source, structure/acquisition, full pose/source and full pose/acquisition were 11.34, 12.07, 16.72 and 13.88 ee points for Fe(P7)Cl, and 9.62, 10.77, 15.70 and 14.27 for Fe(CMC-Por)Cl, respectively. The combined full-pose/acquisition cell improved on full-pose/source, but had higher mean error than both structure-only cells in each system. The source-order-minus-acquisition ordering benefit was −0.73 and −1.15 ee points with structure-only features, versus 2.85 and 1.43 with the full pose block. The difference between ordering benefits (structure-only minus pose) was −3.58 and −2.58 ee points. This indicates a descriptive dependence on representation in this protocol; it does not establish a statistically significant or general interaction.

The full-pose representation here includes all 248 Stage 2p fields and uses Ridge, whereas the original pose representation benchmark used selected descriptors, Elastic Net and different validation protocols. The results should not be treated as a replication or contradiction of the earlier benchmark. We have revised the manuscript and SI to state the different settings and the combined method’s higher error relative to the structure-only cells. The splits overlap and derive from two small retrospective datasets; further validation is needed before generalising the result.
