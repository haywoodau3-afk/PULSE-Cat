# Draft response to Reviewer 1, comment 5

We thank the reviewer for identifying the restriction of the Fe(CMC-Por)Cl acquisition analysis. The published study includes both aryl and arylsulfonyl azide substrate scopes, whereas the original acquisition comparison was defined on the 26 active aryl records; the 17 sulfonyl records in the 50-record curated ledger were excluded. We have clarified that the reported 33.2% reduction applies to the aryl cohort, and that this restriction limits generality. The source reports the aryl scope at −10 °C with Boc2O and the sulfonyl scope at 40 °C, with an additional 25 °C result for 6a. Thus the classes also differ in reported reaction conditions.

To examine the effect of this restriction, we evaluated an expanded candidate pool with the 17 sulfonyl records using 20 overlapping, class-stratified common-test splits, the same two aryl starting substrates for the compared routes and frozen acquisition weights. For uncertainty–diversity, mean held-out aryl MAE was 14.22 ee points with the aryl-only pool and 14.36 with the mixed pool; held-out sulfonyl MAE was 19.03 and 16.97, respectively. The mixed pool gave lower sulfonyl error in 12/20 splits and lower aryl error in 8/20. The source-order route selects no sulfonyl record through budget 20, so it is not a balanced mixed-class comparator. These comparisons change candidate pool and training composition together, and they cannot isolate class, condition or ordering effects.

We also ran a separate sulfonyl-only replay. Mean errors for source order, uncertainty–diversity and random were 17.51, 18.61 and 18.71 ee points, respectively; uncertainty–diversity was not consistently superior to source order. These small retrospective analyses do not establish a general advantage for the expanded scope, and we have revised the manuscript and SI accordingly. We also identified a condition metadata error in the original curated ledger and a <5% yield reported as an exact value for 6q; a revision-local source-corrected copy and the limitations are documented. The distributed data should be corrected before submission.
